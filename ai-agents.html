AI, WITH A PURPOSE

First fix the workflow. Then add intelligence.

AI is most useful when its task is specific, its information boundaries are clear, and people remain in control of consequential decisions. We focus on practical capacity—not novelty.

RESPONSIBLE BOUNDARIES

Useful agents need clear limits.

  • 01Define what the agent may access, produce, and initiate.
  • 02Keep human review for sensitive, financial, or business-critical actions.
  • 03Measure quality and operational value before expanding scope.
  • 04Avoid using AI to conceal a broken or poorly understood process.
